Our Story

Built by
Runners,
For Runners.

Unlaced Theory began with a simple belief: running is better together. What started as a handful of friends lacing up on a run has grown into a thriving, inclusive community of over 200 local runners of every shape, speed, and story.

We don't believe in pace gates or performance benchmarks. Our philosophy is simple — if you put on shoes and show up, you belong here. We've seen first-time joggers become half-marathoners and seasoned athletes rediscover their love for the sport, all because of the community around them.

Every run we host is guided by our core value: no runner left behind.

Inclusive
Walking to sub-8 min miles — every pace is a valid pace.
Community
We run together, recover together, celebrate together.
Supervision
All the runs are supervised by the Police Department.
Safe
Trained leads, lit routes, and a culture of looking out for each other.
