Our Story

Built Just For You.

We’re just two classmates who always wanted to start something of our own. After throwing around a bunch of random ideas and brainstorming for days, we finally ended up creating a run club.

At first, we used to fight over the silliest things, but somewhere along the way, it turned into something really meaningful. Being friends and business partners at the same time honestly feels amazing now.

The whole idea behind starting a run club in Vijayawada was to give teenagers something exciting and positive to look forward to on weekend mornings. Instead of just binge-watching stuff at home, people can come out, socialize, have fun, focus on fitness, and be part of a community.

For us, it’s not just about running — it’s about creating memories, meeting new people, and building a healthier and happier youth culture in the city.

Where It Began

The Genesis: UT Run - 1

Every movement needs a starting point. UT Run - 1 our very first run, was honestly one of the most unforgettable experiences for us. Seeing an idea that started from random conversations actually come to life felt surreal.

The run was held at Bratt, and what made it even more special was the police escort that accompanied us throughout the route. It gave the whole event such an energetic and professional vibe, and seeing everyone run together early in the morning was genuinely exciting.

But it wasn’t just about the run. After crossing the finish line, everyone stayed back for the afterparty, where people got to chill, interact, dance, take pictures, and just enjoy the moment together. Watching strangers become friends in just a few hours made us realize that this was exactly the kind of community we wanted to build in Vijayawada.
